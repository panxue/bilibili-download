# Iteration 0.4.1 — 1080P60 display fix and configurable codec

## Changes

- Quality list grouping | by `height` → by **qn (Bilibili quality code)** | yt-dlp's bilibili extractor reports both 1080P and 1080P high frame rate (qn=80 vs qn=112/116) as `height`/`fps` = 1080/30, indistinguishable; switched to the extractor's unique `quality` (=qn) for grouping, high-frame-rate tier split out as `1080P60`
- Quality labels | `labels(by height)` → `qn_labels` mapping | 80→1080P, 112→1080P60, 116→1080P60, 74→720P60, 120→4K, 127→8K, etc., falling back to height labels
- Codec preference | `sorted()` alphabetical order → `codec_family`/`codec_rank` per `hvc>av01>avc` | `hev1` grouped into hvc (HEVC alias), `-f` expression uses the `[vcodec*=hvc]/[vcodec*=av01]/[vcodec*=avc]/...` chain, verified selecting `30102 hvc1.1.6`
- Configurable codec | hardcoded `CODEC_PRIORITY` → `normalize_codec_order()` | userscript settings panel "codec preference" dropdown (auto/HEVC>AV1>H.264/AV1>HEVC>H.264/H.264 first/HEVC only/H.264 only), stored in `GM_setValue`, submitted with the `codec` field of `POST /api/download`, recorded in job `params`, reused on resume
